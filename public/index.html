      How it works

      • 💤 Nap = 0 wheel passes in the round · 🐾 Jog = 1–499 · 🔥 Zoomies = 500+.
      • You can call any of the next 6 rounds (one hour), and change a call until its round starts. You get 12 calls per UTC day.
      • Points are about 1 ÷ how often that outcome happened over the last week, so guessing blindly averages ~1 point a round. Reading the hamster beats that. This week: —.
      • Results come from SqueekCore on Sepolia: a round's work from its RoundRecorded event, or 0 if a settlement covered it without one. Each result links its transaction.
      • If the wheel feed goes offline during a round, that round is void and scores nothing for anyone.
      • Weekly seasons start Monday 00:00 UTC. Ties go to whoever used fewer calls. Top players may be shared with Squeekchain; their Genesis rules decide any whitelist spots.

      🐹 Squeektoshi's wheel time is never forced or encouraged. This game only predicts what he chooses to do, and naps score too.
